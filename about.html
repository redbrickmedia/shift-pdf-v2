About Shift PDF

Your PDF work stays close to you.

Shift PDF is an experimental toolkit for everyday document jobs. Most tools process files directly in your browser, without an account, a daily quota, or display ads interrupting the task.

Local by default

Your document normally stays in this browser tab while the tool works. Shift does not need the contents of your file to run those local tools.

No surprise gate

The experiment does not ask you to create an account, start a trial, or pay at download time to finish a supported job.

Open and inspectable

Shift PDF builds on BentoPDF and other open-source projects. The corresponding source and license notices are available to every user.

What “local” does and does not mean

Core PDF processing runs with browser technologies such as JavaScript, WebAssembly, and web workers. Some advanced features may download processing modules before they run. A feature that requires a remote service should say so before you use it.

This is an experimental surface. Do not use it for sensitive production documents until the specific tool and its network behavior have been reviewed for your needs.
